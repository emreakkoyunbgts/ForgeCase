REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — Core Banking Modernisation

The Authority seeks a delivery partner to modernise its core banking platform to
support real-time payments. The successful bidder must demonstrate prior experience of:

  - migrating a legacy core banking system without prolonged downtime
  - event-driven architecture, ideally using Kafka
  - delivery in the GCC region for a Tier-1 retail bank
  - reducing end-of-day batch processing windows

Bidders should provide evidence of comparable engagements, including measurable outcomes.
